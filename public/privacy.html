Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

FuelTrak collects the following information to provide our logistics management services:

  • Account Information: Email address, mobile number, company name
  • Transaction Data: Authority to Load (ATL) submissions, truck details, driver information
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, access times for security purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our logistics management services
  • To verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access
  • To communicate with you about your ATL submissions
  • To comply with legal obligations under Philippine law (RA 10173 - Data Privacy Act)

3. Data Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Rate limiting and brute force protection
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of FuelTrak and its users

5. Cookies

FuelTrak uses essential cookies only:

  • Authentication Token: Stored in your browser to maintain your login session
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics

6. Your Rights (GDPR & RA 10173)

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: You can correct inaccurate data
  • Right to Erasure: You can request deletion of your data
  • Right to Object: You can object to data processing
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a portable format

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. ATL records are retained for business and audit purposes.
